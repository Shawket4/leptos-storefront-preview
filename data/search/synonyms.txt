# (whole words only), in either direction. Shop-only synonyms go in synonyms.<shop>.txt.

# - Mix English, standard Arabic and Egyptian Arabic freely, and write each word once: hamza
#   (أ إ آ ا), ة/ه, ى/ي, diacritics, tatweel and case are folded, and the article (ال، بال، وال…)
#   comes off by itself. Plurals ending in ات or -s are stemmed; list other plurals (شنط، اطباق).
# - A multi-word entry ("لاب توب") is matched as one word: «لاب توب» finds laptop and لابتوب.
# - Leave out words that often mean something else in product text: ساعة alone in specs means
#   hours; شراب is a drink and socks; عروسة is a doll and the El Arosa brand; جوز is walnuts but
#   جوز هند is coconut; محمول alone is "portable"; حمام is pigeon and bathroom; قدر is a pot and
#   "amount"; عجلة is a bicycle and a wheel; "soda" is also baking soda; "tee" is also a golf tee.
